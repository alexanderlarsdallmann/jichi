# Academic integrity

Academic integrity means the work you submit is honestly your own, and you give
credit for everything that is not. It is the foundation the whole system rests on,
and breaking it — even by accident — carries real consequences (a failed
assignment, a failed course, worse). Understanding it protects you.

**Plagiarism is presenting someone else's work or ideas as your own.** It includes
the obvious (copying text) and the subtle:

- Paraphrasing an idea without citing it (changing words does not make it yours).
- Reusing your *own* previously-submitted work without permission (self-plagiarism).
- Copying structure or argument even if the words differ.
- "Patchwriting" — stitching together sources with light edits and no citation.

The fix is simple and always the same: **cite your sources, and do your own
thinking.** When in doubt, cite and attribute.

**Using an AI assistant honestly (including this one).** AI writing tools raise a
real, current integrity question, and the responsibility is yours:

- **Know your institution's rules.** They vary widely — some require you to
  disclose AI help, some restrict it to certain tasks, some forbid it for graded
  work. Find your course and university policy and follow it. Not knowing the rule
  is not a defense.
- **Do not submit AI-generated text as your own writing.** Using a tool to plan,
  get feedback, or check citations is different from having it write the paper you
  hand in. This bench is built to *coach* you, not to write for you — that is a
  deliberate line.
- **Disclose when required.** If your institution asks you to state what tools you
  used and how, do so honestly.
- **The learning is the point.** The reason to write the essay is to learn to
  think and argue. Outsourcing that to a tool skips the only part that was ever
  for you.

**The honest test:** could you explain, defend, and have produced every idea and
sentence in your submission yourself? If yes, you are on solid ground. If a part
is not really yours and not credited, fix it before you submit.
