---
description: Set up and update a simple spending log so you can see where the money actually goes — the honest half of budgeting.
---
Help the user track their actual spending in `spending.csv` (a plain file they
own). A budget is a plan; tracking is what tells you if the plan is real. This is
educational, not advice, and the data stays local — remind them to keep full
account numbers and logins out of it and out of the chat.

1. **Set up the log** if it does not exist: a simple CSV with `date,category,
   amount,note`. Categories match the budget so the two can be compared.
2. **Add entries** the user reports, or help them import a batch (a list they
   paste of rounded amounts — not a full raw bank statement). Categorize each,
   asking when unsure rather than guessing.
3. **Keep it low-friction.** The tracker only works if they keep it up, so favour
   quick capture over perfect precision. Rounded is fine.

At any point, total by category and compare to the budget's plan, matter-of-factly
(no judgement about *what* was spent — just where the plan and reality differ, so
they can adjust one or the other). Suggest `/review-spending` for a fuller look.
