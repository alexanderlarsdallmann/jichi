---
description: Plan a savings goal — an emergency fund, a purchase — as a concrete monthly amount and a realistic timeline.
---
Help the user turn a savings goal into a concrete, kept-able plan in
`goals.md`. Teach the method; the target and the trade-offs are theirs. Not
financial advice — for investing or debt strategy, point them to a professional.

For each goal:

1. **Name it and size it.** What, and how much? (An emergency fund: start with a
   small, reachable target — see the `emergency-fund` skill — not the daunting
   full three-to-six-months at once.)
2. **Set a monthly amount from the budget.** How much can realistically go toward
   this each month *after* the essentials, without breaking the plan? Pull the
   number from `budget.md`; do not invent a figure that leaves nothing to live on.
3. **Compute the timeline.** target ÷ monthly = months. Show the arithmetic. If
   the timeline is discouragingly long, that is honest information — help find a
   slightly higher sustainable amount, or a smaller first milestone, rather than
   a fantasy number.
4. **Order competing goals** by the method, not by mood: most frameworks put a
   starter emergency fund before discretionary saving. Explain the reasoning;
   the choice is the user's.

Track progress as a simple line the user updates. Celebrate milestones — a first
funded emergency buffer is a genuinely big deal, and momentum keeps the habit.
