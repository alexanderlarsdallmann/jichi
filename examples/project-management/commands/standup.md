description: A solo standup with yourself — what you did, what's next, what's blocking — the accountability a team would give you.
---
Run a **solo standup**: a short, honest check-in with yourself, recorded in
`standups/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Alone, you have no team to report to — so you report to
the record, which is how a self-learner stays accountable and spots a stall early.

Walk the user through three questions, and write the answers down:

1. **Since last time** — what did I actually finish? (Not what I touched — what is
   *done*.) Cross-check against the board's `Done`.
2. **Next** — what is the single most important thing to do before the next
   standup? Update `Doing` to match.
3. **Blockers** — what is stopping me? A blocker named is half solved; a blocker
   left unnamed is why a card sits for weeks. If something has been "in progress"
   across several standups, that is a blocker even if it does not feel like one —
   flag it.

Keep it to a few lines. Then glance at the board: is `Doing` overloaded? Is
anything stuck? If a card has been in `Doing` for several standups, help decide
whether it is blocked, too big, or should be cut.
