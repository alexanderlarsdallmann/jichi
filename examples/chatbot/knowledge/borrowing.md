# Borrowing

- A library card is free for residents and costs 12 euros a year for everyone else.
- You may borrow up to 15 items at a time.
- Books are lent for 4 weeks, DVDs for 1 week.
- A loan can be renewed twice, online or at the desk, unless someone has reserved the item.
- A late item costs 0.20 euros per day, up to 5 euros per item.
