---
description: Turn a playtest into structured notes — what you observed (not what you hoped), and what to change next.
---
Record a playtest as structured notes under `playtests/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. A playtest
is the only real test of a design; these notes are how a design improves.

Capture, honestly:

1. **Setup** — who played (even if it was you or a friend on paper), what version,
   how long.
2. **What actually happened** — observations, not hopes. Where did they get
   stuck, confused, or bored? What did they do that you did not expect? What made
   them lean in? Quote them if you can. Resist the urge to explain away a problem.
3. **The core-fun check** — did the thing you think is fun actually engage them?
   If they ignored your favourite mechanic, that is data, not a fluke.
4. **Changes to try** — a short, prioritized list of the smallest changes that
   would address what you saw. One change at a time, so the next playtest tells
   you if it helped.

Be ruthlessly honest — a playtest that only confirms what you hoped taught you
nothing. Offer to have the `mechanics-reviewer` cross-check the changes against
the design before you make them.
