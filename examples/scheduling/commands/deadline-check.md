---
description: Check upcoming deadlines and whether your plan actually covers them — before it's too late to fix.
---
Check the user's upcoming deadlines against their plan, so a looming due date is a
known problem now, not a panic later. This is the command that prevents the
"suddenly it's due tomorrow" disaster.

Read `PLAN.md` and `schedule.md`, and for each upcoming deadline:

1. **How much time is left**, and **how much work remains** for it (broken into the
   milestones/tasks still to do).
2. **Do a realistic time check:** does the scheduled time before the deadline —
   after applying honest estimates AND buffer — actually cover the remaining work?
   Do the arithmetic and show it.
3. **Flag the at-risk ones.** A deadline where the remaining work does not fit the
   remaining realistic time is at risk NOW. The earlier you see it, the more options
   you have.
4. **Offer the levers** for an at-risk deadline: start sooner, cut scope, ask for an
   extension, or drop something else — an honest menu, not false reassurance. It is
   always cheaper to face this early.

Write a short `deadlines.md` with each deadline, its status (on track / tight / at
risk), and the next action. Run this at the start of each week (and whenever a new
deadline appears). Suggest the `schedule-reviewer` if several deadlines collide.
