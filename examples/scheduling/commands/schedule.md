---
description: Plan a realistic week — what to do when, working backward from deadlines, with buffer built in.
---
Help the user plan the week ahead in `schedule.md`, realistically. A good weekly
plan is honest about how much fits, not a wish list of everything they hope to do.

Work in this order:

1. **Gather the fixed points.** Commitments already set (classes, appointments,
   work) — block those first; the plan works around them.
2. **List what must happen this week** and, for anything with a **deadline**, work
   *backward*: what has to be done by when to hit it with margin, not against it.
   Pull deadlines from `PLAN.md`.
3. **Estimate each task's time — honestly.** Use the learner's own history if they
   have it (see `/estimate-review`); if a task usually takes 1.5x their guess, plan
   for 1.5x. Optimistic estimates are why weeks fall apart.
4. **Place work in the week, matched to energy.** Hard/creative work in their peak
   hours, admin in the troughs. Real focused blocks for the work that needs them
   (`/timeblock`).
5. **Leave buffer.** Do NOT fill every hour — keep ~20-30% unscheduled for
   overruns, interruptions, and rest. A wall-to-wall plan breaks on the first
   surprise.

Write the week as a simple day-by-day list or table. Keep it light enough to
actually follow. End by naming the one or two most important things this week (what
must not slip), and suggest the read-only `schedule-reviewer` to check it is
realistic before you commit to it.
